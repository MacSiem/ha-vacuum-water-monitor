# Confirmed device calibration and voluntary contributions

The 11 October sprint keeps the existing integration, card, history and reservoir
controls. Its goal is the best evidence-supported estimate for a particular
robot, dock, integration and settings, with explicit unknowns. A replay, nominal
tank capacity and empty signal do not establish physical accuracy.

## Accounting

Record floor area, active-time and dock doses separately, including the resolved
context and provenance. Preserve totals and legacy history; an empty anchor is a
reservoir-level observation, not a new consumption dose. No percentage of a wash
is refunded without a measured volume. Interrupted washes keep an uncertain
estimate and explanation. Unknown settings and observation gaps cannot train a
device correction. Historical nominal factors remain available as legacy data;
new empty events cannot promote them to confirmed measurements.

## Local collection

Use existing full-to-full measurement recording. Before collection the user can
record settings which HA does not expose, together with the dock variant and
integration version. HA values take precedence; unavailable telemetry remains
unknown. These declarations apply prospectively and never fill old history.
Local samples require confirmed matching reservoir levels, no intervening refill
or unmeasured transfer, an instrument and resolution, complete exposure and an
unchanged recorded context. Separate validation samples never change the fit.
Reject duplicate, impossible, incomplete and outlying training samples. Keep
sample counts, measured domain, quality, provenance and validation errors visible.
Whole-cycle measurements cannot identify separate floor and wash rates unless
their recorded exposures provide an identifiable independent design.

## Contributions

Sharing is disabled by default and operates per reviewed contribution. A strict
allowlist creates a technical payload from confirmed samples; no raw histories,
absolute dates, household/device/account identifiers, IP addresses, maps or room
data are exported. Show the exact bytes before separate consent. The existing
maintained GitHub intake is the delivery channel: the user downloads the approved
payload and submits it consciously through the repository's measurement form.
Do not automatically upload current private data or deploy a public endpoint.
Document metadata and re-identification limitations and how to request withdrawal;
public copies may remain. Intake validation and independent maintainer review
precede any promotion into runtime rates; a contribution is not an approved rate.

## Qualification

Targeted RED→GREEN and saved-source replay precede one candidate. Qualify actual
HA consumers, HACS fresh/5.9.1 upgrade, data preservation, rollback, roles,
PL/EN, light/dark, narrow/wide, keyboard, failures and stale UI selections.
Use synthetic fixtures for public tests and share previews. Private traces stay
local. Physical accuracy remains open until independent volume evidence exists.
Release requires coordinator acceptance and an exact-head grant through the guard.
