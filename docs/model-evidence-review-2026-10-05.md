## Action-specific observations

The following quantities are retained as source observations, not runtime coefficients:

- Xiaomi H50 Pro and Robot Vacuum 5 Pro manufacturer FAQs declare approximately 180 mL initial mop washing and 120 mL mid-task washing. Exact meaning of the initial action, final wash, firmware and HA phase binding remain unverified. Sources: [H50 Pro FAQ](https://www.mi.com/pl/support/faq/details/KA-1036331/), [5Pro FAQ](https://www.mi.com/uk/support/faq/details/KA-597420/).
- Qrevo 5AE first-hand review reports approximately 150–160 mL deep washing, with about 220 seconds duration. Apparatus, repetitions and applicability to other washing modes are unverified. [Source](https://www.agdmaniak.pl/124755/roborock-qrevo-5ae-test/amp/).
- Saros 20 first-hand review reports approximately 200–210 mL prewet/mid-task washing and 300 mL final washing in one configured tile-floor scenario. Dirty-water return is not clean-water draw and is not divided by area to make a floor coefficient. Method, repetitions and HA action binding are unverified. [Source](https://www.agdmaniak.pl/131092/roborock-saros-20-test/).

No observation above is automatically applied to every wash or turned into a whole-model physical accuracy claim. The reviewed public vacuum-consumption-data snapshot contains zero approved shared consumption profiles. Missing exact evidence remains unknown; the per-model hardware validation requirement is open.
